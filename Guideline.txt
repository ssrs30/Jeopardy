[Basic Process]
1. Wait for the game to load completely, then enter the start interface.
2. Enter your username, select an avatar, and click Continue to start the game.
3. Players can use in-game gold coins to purchase items and then select questions from the board.
4. The game system displays corresponding issues and initiates a countdown, with the duration decreasing as the difficulty increases.
5. The player clicks the option and competes with the AI player in a quiz.
6. If correct, the player receives that score.If the answer is incorrect, the point will be deducted, and other players can continue answering.
7. Players who answered correctly proceed to the next question.
8. Daily Double appears in a hidden location.There is one in Round 1 and two in Round 2.This question is answered exclusively by the player who discovered it. The player may choose their own bet amount (which must not exceed their total score).
9. After Round 1 and Round 2, the competition proceeds to the Final Round.After all players have placed their bets, answer separately.
10. The game is over.The player's score is converted into in-game gold coins.Click Continue to start the next round.

[Mode of Operation]
Select question: Click the mouse
Quick Answer: After the host finishes reading the question, press the Quick Answer button (spacebar/handle trigger key) immediately.
Enter the answer: Type on the keyboard or select from the prompt.Note that it must be in question form
Place a bet (Daily Double / Final): Enter the amount using the numeric keys and confirm
