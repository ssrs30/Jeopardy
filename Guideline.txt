[Basic Process]
1. Click "START" to enter the game.
2. Enter your username, select a character, and click "CONTINUE" to start the game.
3. When the questions have finished loading, click "CONTINUE" to start the game.
4. On the question board, players can use coins to purchase items and equip them for use. Players can then choose a question from the question board, and the equipped items will be used in that question. If you chose a question with a "Daily Double" (see 7.), equipped items will not be used or consumed. Players can re-equip unused items after answering this question.
5. After selecting a question, you will compete with the other AI players. All players are given 7 seconds to answer. The AI's response speed and accuracy decrease as the number of rounds increases.
6. If a player clicks on an option first, the result will pop up and this question will end. If the answer is correct, the answering player receives the score for the question. If the answer is incorrect, the points will be deducted from the answering player's total points.
7. "Daily Double" appears in a random question. There is one in Single Jeopardy and two in Double Jeopardy. In this question, players can wager the points for the question, which should be between 5 and your current total score. If the current score is below 5, players can only bet 5 points. AI players will be given the same amount in the same question.
8. After Single Jeopardy and Double Jeopardy, all players whose score is greater than 0 can participate in the Final Jeopardy. Participating players can wager their preferred amount between 0 and their total score, and answer the same question separately with no time limit. Players with a score less than or equal to 0 cannot participate nor wager. When all players have answered, the player can click "CONTINUE" to see the result.
9. The game is over when Final Jeopardy ends. The player's score will be converted into in-game coins if it is greater than zero, otherwise the player will not earn any coins.
10. Players with higher scores will receive higher rankings. If two players have the same score, the player with more correct answers ranks higher.
11. If the player is in the first place, their winning streak will accumulate.
12. If the player's score breaks the historical record, the highest score will be updated.
13. Click on “RESTART” to restart the game from Single Jeopary. Click on "BACK TO HOMEPAGE" to return to the homepage.
14. Items, coins, highest records, and consecutive win records purchased will be recorded. To load a saved record, simply enter the same username to continue from the previous save.

Note: Your progress will be saved only after completing an entire game session.

[Shop and Items]
1. Skips (500 coins)
- allows player to skip a question, but no points will be gained or lost
2. Fifty-fifty (500 coins)
- one wrong answer will be removed when used
3. Double award (500 coins)
- player will gain double the points of the question they answered correctly.
- player will lose the original number of points when they answer the question wrongly.
4. Shield (500 coins)
- When used, no loss of points will occur when a question is answered wrongly.

[Mode of Operation]
General:
- Click on buttons and tiles on the screen with the mouse.
- Close the window to exit the program.
- Open the pause window from the top-right corner by clicking on the "X" button.
Homepage:
- Click "START" to begin the game.
- Click "HELP" for the tutorial.
Character screen:
- Click on the username input field, then use the keyboard to enter text (Backspace to delete, normal characters to append).
- Choose one of the four characters.
- Click "CONTINUE" to start the game (a username must be entered).
Loading screen:
- After the question set has finished generating, click "CONTINUE" to proceed.
Turn preparation:
- After the countdown ends, click "CONTINUE" to enter the board.
- "SHOP" & "ITEMS" can be accessed from the top bar.
Question board:
- Click on an unrevealed question tile to choose it.
- "SHOP" & "ITEMS" can be accessed from the top bar.
"Daily Double" / Final Jeopardy – wagering:
- Click on the input field and use the keyboard to enter a number (Backspace to delete).
- Click "CONTINUE" to confirm the wager.
Answering a question:
- Click on one of the three options to buzz in.
Single question result:
- Click "CONTINUE" to proceed.
Shop:
- Click "RETURN" to go back to the previous screen.
- "ITEMS" can be accessed from the top-right.
- Click on any of the four item slots to buy an item.
Items:
- Click "RETURN" to go back to the previous screen.
- "SHOP" can be accessed from the top-right.
- Click on a slot that contains an item to equip it.
Pause:
- "YES" confirms exit from the game.
- "NO" cancels and returns to the current screen.
Game over:
- "RESTART" – keep the character, reset the game, and reload the question set.
- "BACK TO HOMEPAGE" – return to the start screen and restart the entire process from the beginning.
